# 🐷 Piggy Ranch : Marvelous Farming

Welcome to Piggy Paradise, a cozy island life simulator where you manage your own pig ranch, explore a tropical island, and master the art of pig genetics! 🏝️

## 🌟 About the Project
In this game, you inherit a beautiful island dedicated to pig raising. It’s not just about farming; it’s about discovery. From cleaning pens to breeding rare, high-performance pigs for local races, there is always something to do under the island sun.

## 🚀 Key Features

### 🧬 Advanced Breeding & Genetics

The heart of the game lies in its inheritance system.
Attributes: Piglets inherit traits from their parents, including Color, Rarity, Speed, and Special Stats.
Powers: Some pigs may manifest unique powers.
Mutations: Experiment with breeding to discover hidden colors and legendary pig varieties!

### 🚜 Island Management & Farming

Pig Care: Feed your pigs and keep them happy. Don't forget to clean their pens to maintain a healthy environment!
The Fields: Visit the farm area to plant seeds. Grow your own fruits and vegetables to feed your herd or sell for profit.
The Market: Head to the local market to buy essential items, rare seeds, and special objects to improve your ranch.

### 🏁 Island Activities

Pig Racing: Train your fastest pigs and enter them into exciting races to win rewards and prestige.
The Port: Take a break and admire the sea at the island's port, the gateway to your tropical paradise.
Exploration: Navigate between different island zones using a smooth, animated UI menu.

## 🛠️ Technical Overview

Engine: Unity 2022+
Language: C#
Key Systems implemented:
Inventory System: Managed stackable items and money.
Save System: Full persistence of inventory, pig stats, and pen cleanliness.
UI Framework: Animated main menu with smooth transitions and hover effects.
Genetics Engine: Algorithm for trait inheritance and mutation.

## 🎮 How to Play

- Clone the repository:
git clone https://github.com/OneShot666/Piggy-MF-Ranch.git
- Open the project in Unity Hub.
- Load the MainIsland scene
- Press Play and start your rancher journey!
